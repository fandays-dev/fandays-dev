## 👋 Hi, I'm the team behind FanDays

This account builds the products that get supporters out of the living room and into the stadium.
Everything here comes back to one question: **how do we give a fan a perfect day?**

## 🏟️ What FanDays is

FanDays turns "we should go to a game sometime" into a booked trip.
We put the whole match day together, so fans can think about the football and nothing else.

- 🎟️ **Match packages.** A guaranteed ticket plus travel and a hotel, as one package with one price.
- ✈️ **Trips across Europe.** The big leagues, the classic derbies and the grounds every fan wants to visit once.
- 🤝 **Fan experiences.** Stadium tours, hospitality, group trips, and days built around a club or a moment.
- 👨‍👩‍👧 **For everyone who loves the game.** First-time visitors, lifelong season-ticket holders, friends, families and companies.

## 💡 What I care about

| | |
|---|---|
| **Simple** | Finding and booking a trip should take minutes, not evenings. |
| **Honest** | One clear price, and no surprises at checkout or at the gate. |
| **Personal** | Every trip is somebody's big day. We treat it that way. |
| **Reliable** | When the whistle blows, everything around it has to just work. |

## 🔭 What I'm working on

Making it easier to find, book and enjoy football trips and fan days,
from the first click to the moment you take your seat.
